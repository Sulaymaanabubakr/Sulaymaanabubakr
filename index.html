Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about courses, mentorship, payments, and support.

How do I enrol for a course?

Go to Courses, Register Now, pick your device type, select a course, and pay securely via Paystack. You’ll get a confirmation and a WhatsApp hand-off.

Which courses are smartphone-friendly?

Graphic Design (Canva, Pixellab), Video Editing (CapCut, Alight Motion, InShot), Social Media Management, Content Writing, Copywriting, AI Productivity, and Ghostwriting.

Do I get a certificate after completing a course?

Yes, students receive a digital certificate and practical project evaluation.

How long does mentorship run?

Mentorship is flexible (e.g., 4–12 weeks). Pick a duration on the registration page and we’ll tailor it to your goals.

Can I pay in installments?

Installments are available for select programs such as web development, app development and UI/UX design. You can choose the option when registering.

What payment methods do you accept?

Paystack supports Cards, Bank Transfer, USSD, and more (based on availability).

Will I need a laptop?

Many courses are smartphone-friendly. Advanced development and design courses are best on a laptop.

What if I miss a session?

We will reschedule where possible. You’ll also get guidance and materials to catch up.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis before a program starts. After access is granted, refunds generally aren’t available.

How can I contact support?

Use the “Let’s Build Together” form or message me on WhatsApp.
